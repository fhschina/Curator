## Conclusion

Default thinking improves the state controls, and its valid final judgments match the frozen expectations in this selected set. Under the existing total completion budget it yields fewer valid correct results overall, many truncated attempts and a substantial cost increase. Keep the current delivered configuration. The next useful step is a bounded test of thinking effort or output budget before expanding to the full reference set. This result does not establish a model capability ceiling.
