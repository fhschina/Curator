## Conclusion

Thinking ON produced 393/480 (81.88%) correct directions and 186/240 (77.50%) correct whole pairs; OFF produced 398/480 (82.92%) and 187/240 (77.92%). All invalid and unresolved judgments remain in the fixed denominators and receive zero credit.

Across the primary runs, ON had 15 invalid personal-reference pairs and 27 length-limited calls. It used 5.87 times the native run time and 7.24 times the completion tokens of OFF.

The primary two-repeat full-set mean does not reach both targets. The original frozen development acceptance is incomplete. The delivered example retains the expanded budget and keeps thinking disabled by default. Individual runs, worst results and every missing verification requirement are reported below. These exposed references do not establish independent human accuracy.

In the primary ON arm, main produced 406/480 correct directions and final produced 393/480. Raw wrong blocks changed from 47 to 61; invalid pairs changed from 12 to 15. These layer snapshots separate the main judgment from changes introduced by later checks.
