## Conclusion

In this fixed diagnostic, thinking ON reached 125/128 (97.66%) correct directions and 62/64 (96.88%) correct whole pairs; OFF reached 111/128 (86.72%) and 52/64 (81.25%). Every invalid judgment remains in the denominator and receives zero credit.

Compared with the archived 6144-budget ON run, length-limited calls fell from 60 to 1 and invalid final pairs from 14 to 1. In the new cohort, ON used 3.72 times the native run time and 5.95 times the completion tokens of OFF. These observations justify a full personal-reference evaluation with thinking at the expanded budget; they do not establish the 90/85 target on the full 120-pair set. The 8 Jinja policy files remain unchanged, and thinking remains disabled by default.

Remaining ON failures: R33 in repeat 1 had a valid judgment with a false block; D058 in repeat 2 ended with an invalid incomplete judgment. The original result is retained without replacement.
